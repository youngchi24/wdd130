Los Dientes del Diablo

Challenging Adventure

Experience an exciting river journey featuring powerful rapids and changing water conditions. This adventure is designed for visitors looking for a more demanding outdoor experience and who are comfortable following a guide's instructions. Preparation, teamwork, and attention to safety are important throughout the journey.

  • Suggested duration: Full day
  • Experience: Challenging
  • Focus: Rapids and teamwork

Intermediate Adventure

Enjoy a memorable river outing combining exciting water sections with opportunities to appreciate the surrounding scenery. Big Mallard is presented as an intermediate adventure for visitors seeking a balance between excitement, outdoor exploration, and learning to work together on the water.

  • Focus: Exploration and teamwork
